## Who Was Kukulkan?

Kukulkan (also known as Quetzalcoatl in Aztec mythology) was the Feathered Serpent — one of the most revered and feared gods in all of Mesoamerican religion. His name comes from the Yucatec Mayan words *k'uk'* (feather) and *kaan* (serpent).

He ruled over wind, rain, storms, and the cycle of creation itself. Without Kukulkan, there would be no rain — and without rain, no maize, no cacao, no life.

## Kukulkan at Chichen Itza

The most famous monument to Kukulkan is the pyramid at Chichen Itza — El Castillo — built so that twice a year during the equinox, sunlight creates a shadow of a serpent descending the pyramid's steps. The Maya engineered this effect deliberately, over 1,000 years ago.
