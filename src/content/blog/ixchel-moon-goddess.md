## Who Was Ixchel?

Ixchel (also spelled Ix Chel) was the principal moon goddess of the ancient Maya — a complex and multifaceted deity who ruled over medicine, healing, weaving, fertility, and the cycles of the moon.

Unlike many moon deities in other cultures, Ixchel was not merely gentle. She had two forms: a young, radiant goddess associated with fertility and creation, and an older, fierce form associated with floods and destruction. The Maya understood that the moon — like life — contains both light and shadow.

## The Island of Cozumel

Ixchel's primary temple was on the island of Cozumel, off the Yucatan coast. Maya women made pilgrimages across open water to reach her shrine, seeking blessings for fertility, safe childbirth, and healing. The island was considered sacred ground.
