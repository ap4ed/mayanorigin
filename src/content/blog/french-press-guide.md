## Why French Press Is Worth Learning

The French press is the most forgiving and most unforgiving brewer at the same time. Forgiving because there's no paper filter stealing your coffee's oils and body. Unforgiving because every mistake — wrong grind, wrong temp, wrong timing — shows up clearly in the cup.

Done right, French press produces the richest, most full-bodied coffee you can brew at home.

## What You Need

- French press (any size)
- Coarsely ground coffee
- Water just off the boil (95–96°C / 203–205°F)
- Timer
- Kitchen scale (strongly recommended)

## The Ratio

Use **1:15 coffee to water by weight**.

- 2-cup press: 20g coffee → 300ml water
- 4-cup press: 34g coffee → 510ml water
- 8-cup press: 55g coffee → 825ml water

## Step-by-Step

1. **Preheat** your press with hot water. Dump it out.
2. **Grind coarse** — like coarse sea salt. This is the most important step. Too fine = bitter, muddy, hard to press.
3. **Add coffee** to the empty press.
4. **Start timer** and pour all the water in at once. Make sure all grounds are wet.
5. **Stir once** gently, then place the lid on (plunger up).
6. **Steep 4 minutes** exactly.
7. **Plunge slowly** — 20–30 seconds of steady, even pressure. If it's too hard to push, your grind is too fine.
8. **Pour immediately** — don't let it sit or it will over-extract.
